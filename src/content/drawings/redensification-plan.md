The redensification plan concentrates 2,200 new housing units along the boulevard, capped at four stories and designed to house the entire displaced population within a walkable core.

### Typology breakdown

| Type | Share | Acreage | Units | Density |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Townhomes & duplexes | 25% | 30 ac | 710 | 15 units/ac |
| Stacked apartments / walk-ups | 55% | 42 ac | 1,050 | 25 units/ac |
| Mixed-use / courtyard | 10% | 12 ac | 220 | 18 units/ac |

Three density districts organize the zone: **D1** (townhomes and walk-ups), **D2** (mid-rise apartments), and **D3** (duplexes and triplexes), ensuring a graduated transition from the boulevard core to the neighborhood edge.
