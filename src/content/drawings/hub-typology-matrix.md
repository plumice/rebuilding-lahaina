The four community hubs are sized and programmed to serve daily life and convert to emergency operations when needed. Each hub combines education, health, and emergency response under one roof so that no single-use facility sits empty during normal conditions.

### Hub types

1. **Hub 1: Primary School / Neighborhood Center**: daily education and recreation; emergency shelter and coordination point.
2. **Hub 2: Health Service / Neighborhood Center / First Response Shelter**: primary care, mental health services, senior programs; emergency medical triage.
3. **Hubs 3 and 4: First Response / Neighborhood Center** (two sites, north and south ends of the core): first response, neighborhood meetings; emergency staging and supply distribution.

### Spatial distribution logic

The hubs are positioned so that every residence in the redensified core falls within a 10-minute walk of at least one hub. Overlapping service radii provide redundancy during evacuation or infrastructure failure.
