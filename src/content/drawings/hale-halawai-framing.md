Reference from County of Maui Rules §15-110, Rules Pertaining to Indigenous Hawaiian Architecture Structures. The structural hierarchy of the hale maps directly to the module system: the relationship between pou (posts), lohelau (wall plate), and oʻa (rafters) informs the post-and-beam hierarchy of the 26-foot module. Source: County of Maui Rules §15-110. Available at https://www.mauicounty.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4434/Indigenous-Hawaiian-Architecture--Structures
