Each module captures rainwater through sloped roofs directing flow into gutters between connected units. A custom Y-shaped column connector at valley gutters merges structural and drainage functions. Subsurface volcanic gravel beds and storage tanks beneath module clusters capture water for non-potable reuse.

### Performance data

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Roof catchment area | 57,460 sq ft |
| Collection efficiency | 80% |
| Annual yield | **429,801 gallons** |
| Daily average | ~1,178 gallons |
| Demand offset | 12–26% |
| Recommended storage | 160,000–250,000 gallons |

This on-site harvesting is a regulatory necessity: West Maui's Stage 2 water shortage prevents new water meter processing and restricts irrigation to one day per week. The school's catchment demonstrates how public architecture contributes to distributed water infrastructure.
