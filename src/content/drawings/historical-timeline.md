A visual timeline tracing the key events that shaped Lahaina across two centuries.

### Key inflection points

- **1795** — Kamehameha conquers Maui, consolidating rule and marking the beginning of Lahaina's integration into a unified Hawaiian kingdom.
- **1802–1823** — Lahaina serves as royal capital, a significant political and spiritual center for Hawaiian chiefs.
- **1831–1834** — Lahainaluna Seminary founded; the first Hawaiian-language newspaper is printed, marking a key development in Hawaiian media and literacy.
- **1850s** — Whaling boom draws sailors and merchants; early urban tensions emerge.
- **1901** — Pioneer Mill starts large-scale sugar production, transforming the labor and land-use landscape for the next century.
- **1962** — Lahaina designated a National Historic Landmark; preservation efforts intensify.
- **1980s–2000s** — Sugar declines, tourism takes over as the dominant industry, reshaping the town's economy and built form.
- **2023** — The wildfire destroys much of Lahaina's historic core, marking a catastrophic rupture and sparking recovery efforts.
